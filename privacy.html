Privacy

What happens to your photos and your number, in plain language.

What we collect when you upload photos

The photographs you choose, your name, your phone number, and anything you type in the notes or size fields. Nothing else. We do not ask for an email address and we do not create an account.

Where it goes

Uploaded photos are stored on Cloudflare's servers, in a private bucket that only this studio can read. They are not published, not indexed by search engines, and there is no public link to them.

How long we keep them

Thirty days after you send them, the files are deleted automatically. If you want them gone sooner, call or message us and we will delete them that day — you do not need a reason.

What we never do

We do not sell your photographs or your number. We do not use your photographs to advertise anything. We do not put them in a catalogue, on social media, or in an example for another customer.

WhatsApp

If you send photos on WhatsApp instead, that conversation lives on WhatsApp and is covered by their privacy policy as well as ours. We keep the chat for as long as we need it to print your order.

On your own device

Your language choice, light or dark theme, and a list of the references you have ordered are saved in your own browser, so the site remembers you. They never leave your phone.

Cookies and tracking

No advertising cookies, and no trackers that follow you to other websites. We do count visits, using Cloudflare Web Analytics — cookieless, no profile of you, and the reason this site needs no cookie banner. That is the whole of it: we know roughly how many people looked at the price list, not who you are.
