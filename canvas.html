Canvas prints — questions

Which sizes do canvas prints come in?

30 × 40 cm · 40 × 60 cm · 60 × 80 cm · 80 × 120 cm.

How much does it cost?

From 950 ETB to 4,600 ETB, depending on size. More than one gets a discount: 2+ items, 10% off; 5+ items, 15% off; 10+ items, 22% off. Prices depend on paper and finish — we confirm the exact price when you order.

How long does it take?

Canvas prints are ready in 1–2 working days.

What size photo file do I need for the largest one?

For 80 × 120 cm at 300 dpi you would need about 9,450 × 14,150 pixels, which no phone camera makes — that is normal for large prints. Send the biggest original you have, as a Document rather than a Photo, and we will tell you honestly how it will look at that size before we print it.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is free in Bole and the surrounding areas. Elsewhere in Addis we deliver for a small fee — just ask when you order.

How do I pay?

Telebirr, bank transfer, or cash at the studio.

What if something is wrong with my print?

If it is our mistake, we reprint it at no cost. Bring it back and we will fix it.
