Colour tokens

Flag yellow is ornament only — it is unreadable as text on cream. Red used for text is --red, not --flag-red. Re-measure contrast after any change.

Accent roles

Canvas prints Photo books Wall calendars

One accent per product, carried on the woven trim and the hero badge. Never a third tricolour — see DESIGN.md §2.

The motif

Ceremonial silk selvedge — the only place all three flag colours appear together (with the progress bar).

Spacing & motion

Scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32. Easing: only --ease-out and --ease-inout. UI feedback <300ms, entrances 420–520ms, carousel 620ms, ribbon 950ms. Transform and opacity only. Three entrance behaviours by element type (blur for headings, scale for cards, rise for prose), 70ms sibling stagger. Everything off under prefers-reduced-motion; autoplay disabled, not just sped up.
